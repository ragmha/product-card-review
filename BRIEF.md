# Product card brief

The product card appears on category pages and in search results. It gives a
shopper enough to decide whether to open the product or add it to the cart.

## Requirements

1. Show the product image, name, a short description, the price and the stock
   status.
2. The image and the name link to the product page.
3. When a product is on sale, show the sale price and the original price struck
   through.
4. Show "Only N left" when five or fewer remain, and "In stock" otherwise.
5. When stock is 0, show "Out of stock" and disable Add to cart.
6. Shoppers can save a product to their wishlist from the card.
7. Merchants supply product names, descriptions, images and links through the
   catalog API. Descriptions may contain basic formatting, such as bold text.
8. The card ships in English first, then in German and Arabic.

## Out of scope

Ratings, product variants and quantity selection.
